Better Files is a file manager with meaning search. Find documents and photos by what is in them, not only by their name.

Meaning search:
- Search documents and photos by meaning.
- The search index and the search model stay on your phone. Your files, file names and searches are never sent.
- Indexing runs in the background, and overnight while the phone is not in use.

File manager:
- Copy, move, rename, delete, share and bookmark files.
- Root support: view and manage files with root access.
- Archive support: view, extract and create common archive files.
- Network storage: FTP, SFTP, SMB and WebDAV.
- Themes, night mode and true black.

Privacy:
- No account and no ads.
- Analytics are off until you turn them on.

Better Files is open source under GPL-3.0.
